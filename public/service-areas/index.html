Service areas

Fort Mohave and the river communities around it.

Staying close to home keeps estimates quick and schedules flexible.

Fort Mohave

Our home base. We coat garages, carports and patios throughout Fort Mohave, including golf-course neighborhoods around Desert Lakes and Los Lagos, homes on the mesas and properties along the Highway 95 corridor. Commercial work here ranges from small service shops to medical and retail buildings.

Bullhead City

North along the river, Bullhead City has a wide mix of homes, from older slabs that need extra preparation to newer construction with builder-sealed garages. We also work with shops, restaurants and storage businesses throughout the city.

Mohave Valley

East and south of Fort Mohave, Mohave Valley includes larger rural lots with detached shops, RV garages and equipment barns. Those buildings often carry heavier loads, so we pay close attention to slab condition and choose heavier systems where needed.

Mesquite Creek

Just south of Fort Mohave, Mesquite Creek homes and outbuildings receive the same full preparation and system options as any project closer in.

Golden Shores & Topock

Farther south toward the Interstate 40 crossing, these river communities include homes with boat storage, carports and outdoor living areas that benefit from UV-stable, textured coatings.

Not sure if you’re in range?

Send us your location with your request. For larger residential and commercial projects we travel throughout the surrounding area. Every service — including garage floor coatings, patio coatings and warehouse floors — is available across our service area.
